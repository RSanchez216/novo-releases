Novo

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Novo is a desktop app for Windows. This policy explains what data Novo uses, where it is kept and who it is shared with.

Data Novo accesses

  • Your Google account email address, to show which account is connected.
  • Your Gmail messages, labels and drafts, through the Gmail API, only to show your email in the app and to do what you ask: read, reply, send, label, archive, move to trash, save drafts and notify you of new email.
  • Tasks and settings you create in Novo.

Where your data is stored

Novo has no servers of its own. Your sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on your computer using the operating system's secure storage. Your tasks and settings are stored on your computer. Your email stays in your Gmail account; Novo does not keep its own copy apart from what is needed to display it.

Sharing

  • Novo does not sell your data and does not use it for advertising.
  • Novo does not share your data with anyone, except: if you choose to use the optional AI features (writing help or inbox summaries), the text needed for that request is sent to Anthropic's API, using an API key you provide, only when you click an AI button or ask for a summary.
  • Novo does not use your Gmail data to train AI models.

Google user data

Novo's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your control

  • Sign out of an account in Novo at any time; this removes its tokens from your computer.
  • Remove Novo's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling Novo and deleting its data folder removes everything Novo stored on your computer.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the support email address shown on Novo's Google sign-in screen.
