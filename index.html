Novo

Your email, tasks and work apps in one window

Novo is a Windows desktop app that brings several Gmail accounts, Google Drive, Calendar, Sheets and other work websites together in one place, organized into spaces with tabs.

What it does

  • Reads, replies to, labels and archives email in several Gmail accounts from one app.
  • Turns emails into tasks that stay linked to the original email, with due dates, priorities and steps.
  • Shows desktop notifications for new email in your Primary inbox.
  • Optional AI writing help and inbox summaries that run only when you ask for them.

Who it is for

Novo is currently used internally and is not offered to the public.
